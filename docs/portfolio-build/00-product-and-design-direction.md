# 00 · Product and design direction

Source of truth: [BRIEF.md](BRIEF.md) (the owner's build brief, copied verbatim).

## Positioning

A software engineer moving into security, with **Application Security**
first, then **Cloud Security** and **DevSecOps**. The site presents the
person and their engineering work first; the interactive Threatscape section
(M2–M4) supports that story and must not overwhelm it.

## Design idea

Experimental editorial design × cybersecurity × infrastructure engineering:
strong typography, asymmetric grids, a deep-neutral base, and colour used
semantically (see [02-visual-system.md](02-visual-system.md)).

## Hard constraints carried from the brief

- No neon-green terminal identity, Matrix rain, fake terminals, random
  padlocks, excessive glow or glassmorphism.
- No fake scanning animations, fabricated statistics, fake security scores,
  or claims without real implementation or data behind them.
- No visitor fingerprinting, device or network scanning, or covert profiling
  ([04-security-and-privacy-decisions.md](04-security-and-privacy-decisions.md)).
- Existing working functionality is preserved.
